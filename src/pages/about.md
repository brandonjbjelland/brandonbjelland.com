# cat about.md

![Brandon on a beach, grinning and saluting in a NASA cap and an astronaut t-shirt](/brandon.jpg)

I’m a platform and agentic engineering leader based in St Paul, Minnesota.

Over the past few years my own workflow has shifted toward agentic development, and that shapes what
I want to build next: **software factories**, where agents and people share one path from idea to
production. I’m most interested in the interfaces that make this work, both for product developers and
for ambitious builders inside the enterprise.

Underneath that is more than a decade of cloud infrastructure and platform engineering. Most recently
I was the architect and technical owner of the platform under every Prisma AIRS product at Palo Alto
Networks, which I joined through the Protect AI acquisition. Before that I led field engineering at
Onix, and spent six years running my own consultancy, Run at Scale, mostly embedded in Google
Professional Services on large GCP deployments. I started out in network operations at Big Fish Games
and the 2010 Vancouver Olympics.

What I want to build next is the **agentic-first future of infrastructure as code**: agents that
propose, plan, and ship infrastructure changes alongside people, through the same reviewed and
versioned path. Getting there doesn’t mean throwing out the past. I lean on a lot of boring but
effective methods of yesteryear, because they’re the foundations that keep teams and agents safe.

## How I work

I adapt to the situation, but I’m generally biased toward:

- **Doing over analyzing.** Production-quality deliverables are the goal. I study the problem space but dive in quickly to learn the domain.
- **Showing over telling.** If a picture is worth a thousand words, a demo speaks volumes.
- **Communicating in multiple mediums.** Code, talks, docs, diagrams, decision trees, and slide decks. Ideas are only as useful as your ability to communicate them.

## Outside of work

I love nothing more than creating. Sometimes that’s tinkering with tech to build something cool or
novel; sometimes it’s drawing with pastels or painting oil on canvas. For me, the creative process is
everything.

I’m a husband and a dad to two girls, and they get most of my time away from the keyboard.

I grew up an athlete and a wrestler (now recovering), and I never really stopped training. I’ve run
half marathons, done powerlifting, and carried grappling into adulthood through Brazilian jiu-jitsu.
In my fifteen years in Seattle I also had a stint in men’s and co-ed roller derby, and skied my buns
off across the Northwest and beyond. I’m also a scuba diver, a Technician class ham radio operator, and an aspiring pilot.
