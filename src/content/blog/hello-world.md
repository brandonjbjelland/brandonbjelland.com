## Writing a post

Each post is a Markdown file in `src/content/blog/`. The frontmatter at the top sets the title,
description, and date, and everything below it is regular Markdown:

```md
---
title: My post
description: One-line summary for lists, RSS, and link previews.
pubDate: 2026-11-04
tags: [platform, ai]
draft: false
---
```
